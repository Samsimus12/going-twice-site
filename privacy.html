Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Going Twice! ("the app") is a mobile game developed by Sam Morrison. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Your game (your shop, stock, cash, progress and settings) is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to any server. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Advertising

Going Twice! shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. An ad only plays when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus; the game never shows banner ads or ads you didn't ask for. AdMob may collect and use data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. the IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and ad interaction data

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their policies. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

Your Choices

  • Tracking: on iOS the app asks for permission before any tracking. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalized ads: you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Consent (EU, UK and other regions where it applies): the app asks for your ad consent on first launch, and you can change it later from Settings → Privacy choices inside the game.
  • No ads at all: simply don't tap "Watch a short ad". The game plays fully without them.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
