Common questions

How do I learn the basics again?
Open Settings (the gear) and tap How to play, Shop tour, or Replay tutorial. Replaying the tutorial keeps your own shop safe.

Is my progress backed up?
Your game is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your shop.

Do I have to watch ads?
No. Ads are only offered as optional bonuses, and the whole game can be played without them.
